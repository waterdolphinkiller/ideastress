Meta / TikTok Paid Social Funnel (CPM)

Convert Meta/TikTok CPM, ad CTR, and landing-page CVR into real CPA and first-order net profit.

How to Use & Exact Formula

Transparent deterministic math — inspect every formula and worked example before trusting any projection.

3-Step Quick Guide

  1. Enter your platform CPM (cost per 1,000 ad impressions, typically $12–$30 in the US) and ad Click-Through Rate (CTR %).
  2. Input your landing-page conversion rate (CVR %), Average Order Value (AOV), and pre-ad gross margin %.
  3. See your exact effective CPC, implied Paid Social CPA, Break-Even ROAS, and first-order profit or loss.

Formula & Worked Example

Effective_CPC = CPM / (1000 × CTR)
Implied_Social_CPA = Effective_CPC / Landing_Page_CVR
First_Order_Net = (AOV × Pre_Ad_Margin_Pct) − Implied_Social_CPA

At $18 CPM and 1.2% CTR (12 clicks per 1,000 impressions), effective CPC is $1.50. At 2.2% landing-page conversion, implied CPA is $68.18. On a $68 AOV product with 60% margin ($40.80 pre-ad profit), you lose $27.38 on every first order — requiring higher CTR creative, bundles to lift AOV, or strong repeat LTV.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why calculate paid social from CPM instead of CPC?

Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and TikTok charge by impressions (CPM). Your real CPC is entirely determined by creative thumb-stop and click-through rate (CTR).

What is a realistic Meta Ads CTR and landing-page CVR?

Cold paid social campaigns commonly see 0.8%–1.8% link CTR and 1.4%–2.7% Shopify store conversion rate (Littledata 2026 median is 1.4%).

Which lever improves paid social CPA fastest?

Doubling creative CTR from 0.9% to 1.8% cuts your effective CPC and CPA in half immediately without changing your product price or website.

Can a store survive negative first-order profit on Meta Ads?

Only if you have verified 60-to-90-day repeat purchase LTV and enough working capital cash to float the payback period without running dry.
