Frequently Asked Questions

What is Geo-Arbitrage in SaaS and indie hacking?

It is launching into non-US or non-English markets where Google Ads CPC and competition are 2x–4x lower than the US while local willingness to pay still supports profitable unit economics.

Why is US Google Ads CPC so high in 2026?

Publicly tracked indie SaaS products roughly tripled between 2020 and 2026 and CAC rose ~60% over five years, driving US high-intent B2B keywords to $6–$15+ per click.

Does IdeaStress support live multi-country CPC lookups?

Yes. With a DataForSEO key in `.env`, `ideastress geo-scan` queries live Google Ads search volume and CPC by country `location_code` and caches results for 30 days in SQLite.

Should I adjust pricing when entering lower-CPC countries?

Yes. Our scanner automatically scales your localized max viable CAC by a purchasing-power factor so you do not overestimate revenue in lower-ARPA regions.
