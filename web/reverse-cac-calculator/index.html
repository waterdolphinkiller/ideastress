Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Reverse Unit-Economics Survival Frontier?

Instead of guessing 3-year revenue, a reverse solver works backward from your cash budget, price, and fixed costs to compute the exact maximum CAC, minimum price floor, and maximum churn your business can survive over 12 months.

How does the 1,000-run Monte Carlo simulation work?

It runs 1,000 reproducible seeded paths perturbing monthly acquisition volume, upward-skewed CAC overruns, variable cost drift, and a realistic Month 1–2 SaaS onboarding churn spike to output survival probability and P10/P50/P90 cash percentiles.

Why did my profitable SaaS get a CHANNEL_IMPOSSIBLE warning?

Even when unit margins are strong, crowded Google Ads categories (e.g., $8.90 CPC at 2.5% CVR = $356 implied CAC) can exceed your solved Max Viable CAC ceiling. That means you must use SEO, outbound, or lower-CPC geos instead of generic search ads.

Does IdeaStress include Stripe US fees and taxes?

Yes. You can toggle Stripe US (2.9% + $0.30), Stripe US + Billing (3.6% + $0.30), or Merchant of Record Paddle/Lemon Squeezy (5% + $0.50), plus an optional effective income tax reserve on positive monthly profit.
