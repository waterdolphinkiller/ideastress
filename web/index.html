What is a Reverse Unit-Economics Survival Frontier?

How does the 1,000-run Monte Carlo simulation work?

01 · 3-STEP QUICK GUIDE
  1. Enter the annual net salary you want to replace (e.g., $120,000) and your estimated US tax reserve % (federal + state + self-employment).
  2. Add your monthly software, hosting, and marketing costs, plus your average monthly subscription price.
  3. Toggle between Stripe US (2.9% + $0.30) and MoR Paddle/Lemon Squeezy (5% + $0.50) to see required MRR and exact annual fee savings.
02 · DETERMINISTIC FORMULA & WORKED EXAMPLE

To replace a $120,000/yr net salary ($10,000/mo) with a 30% tax reserve ($14,286 pre-tax profit needed) and $1,500 monthly ops on a $49/mo Stripe US plan ($47.28 net/cust), you need 334 paying customers = $16,366 MRR. Using Stripe US saves ~$4,925/yr in fees vs a 5% + $0.50 MoR.

03 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How much MRR do I need to replace a $100,000 salary in the US?

After setting aside ~30% for US federal/state/self-employment taxes, payment processing fees, and modest operating costs, replacing $100k net typically requires $13,000–$15,000 in stable MRR.

Should I use Stripe or a Merchant of Record (Paddle / Lemon Squeezy)?

Stripe US charges 2.9% + $0.30 (cheaper on fees), while Paddle/Lemon Squeezy charge 5% + $0.50 but act as the legal seller handling global VAT and US sales tax compliance for you.

Why shouldn't I quit my job the first month I hit target MRR?

Early SaaS MRR has high churn volatility. Wait until you have held target MRR for 3+ consecutive months and banked 6–12 months of personal emergency runway.

Is this formal US tax advice?

No. Tax reserve percentages are planning estimates only; consult a licensed CPA for LLC/S-Corp elections and quarterly estimated tax filings.

03 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

When does a Freemium model fail for AI SaaS?

Freemium fails whenever free users consume recurring LLM tokens or GPU compute without driving viral word-of-mouth — even $0.50/mo per free user destroys margins at a typical 1.5%–2% conversion rate.

What is a realistic free-to-paid conversion rate?

Self-serve B2B/prosumer freemium products typically convert 1%–5% of free signups; assuming >8% without strong usage gates is usually overly optimistic.

How do I fix a bleeding freemium tier?

Replace unlimited free access with a one-time free credit allowance (e.g., 10 lifetime credits), a 7–14 day reverse trial, or strict client-side/zero-cost free tools.

Why does Stripe fee apply only to paid users?

Payment processing fees (2.9% + $0.30) are incurred only on paid transactions, while infrastructure and AI token costs apply to both free and paid cohorts.

Meta / TikTok Paid Social Funnel (CPM)

Convert Meta/TikTok CPM, ad CTR, and landing-page CVR into real CPA and first-order net profit.

METHODOLOGY & FAQ 3-Step Guide, Exact Formula, Worked Example & 4 FAQs +
01 · 3-STEP QUICK GUIDE
  1. Enter your platform CPM (cost per 1,000 ad impressions, typically $12–$30 in the US) and ad Click-Through Rate (CTR %).
  2. Input your landing-page conversion rate (CVR %), Average Order Value (AOV), and pre-ad gross margin %.
  3. See your exact effective CPC, implied Paid Social CPA, Break-Even ROAS, and first-order profit or loss.
02 · DETERMINISTIC FORMULA & WORKED EXAMPLE
Effective_CPC = CPM / (1000 × CTR)
Implied_Social_CPA = Effective_CPC / Landing_Page_CVR
First_Order_Net = (AOV × Pre_Ad_Margin_Pct) − Implied_Social_CPA

At $18 CPM and 1.2% CTR (12 clicks per 1,000 impressions), effective CPC is $1.50. At 2.2% landing-page conversion, implied CPA is $68.18. On a $68 AOV product with 60% margin ($40.80 pre-ad profit), you lose $27.38 on every first order — requiring higher CTR creative, bundles to lift AOV, or strong repeat LTV.

03 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Why calculate paid social from CPM instead of CPC?

Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and TikTok charge by impressions (CPM). Your real CPC is entirely determined by creative thumb-stop and click-through rate (CTR).

What is a realistic Meta Ads CTR and landing-page CVR?

Cold paid social campaigns commonly see 0.8%–1.8% link CTR and 1.4%–2.7% Shopify store conversion rate (Littledata 2026 median is 1.4%).

Which lever improves paid social CPA fastest?

Doubling creative CTR from 0.9% to 1.8% cuts your effective CPC and CPA in half immediately without changing your product price or website.

Can a store survive negative first-order profit on Meta Ads?

Only if you have verified 60-to-90-day repeat purchase LTV and enough working capital cash to float the payback period without running dry.

03 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Why do AI SaaS products have lower gross margins than traditional SaaS?

Traditional private SaaS averages ~80% gross margin (Benchmarkit 2026), whereas AI apps incur recurring per-token inference COGS on every user prompt, often compressing margins to 40%–65%.

What is the Whale Bankruptcy Limit?

It is the exact number of daily requests at which a heavy power user on a flat-rate 'unlimited' subscription costs more in LLM API fees than their entire monthly payment.

How can I protect margins on an AI wrapper?

Route routine prompts to budget/mini models, enforce hard max_tokens caps, cache repeated context, and gate frontier reasoning or video generation behind credit packs or add-on tiers.

Can IdeaStress scan my actual repository for token cost risks?

Break-Even Calculator (US Stripe Ready)

Exact units and revenue required to cover monthly fixed overhead after payment fees.

METHODOLOGY & FAQ 3-Step Guide, Exact Formula, Worked Example & 4 FAQs +
01 · 3-STEP QUICK GUIDE
  1. Select your payment processor preset (Stripe US 2.9% + $0.30, MoR 5% + $0.50, or None).
  2. Enter your selling price, variable cost per unit, refund rate, and total monthly fixed overhead.
  3. Compare your expected monthly unit volume against the solved Break-Even Units to see monthly operating profit or shortfall.
02 · DETERMINISTIC FORMULA & WORKED EXAMPLE
Net_Unit_Contribution = Selling_Price × (1 − Refund_Rate) − Variable_Cost − Payment_Fee
Break_Even_Units = ceil(Fixed_Monthly_Costs / Net_Unit_Contribution)

At a $65 selling price, 2% refund rate ($63.70 net), $22 variable cost, and $2.19 Stripe US fee, net unit contribution is $39.51. Covering $4,200 in fixed monthly overhead requires ceil(4200 / 39.51) = 107 units/month ($6,955 monthly revenue).

03 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do Stripe fees affect break-even units?

Stripe's US domestic card fee of 2.9% + $0.30 reduces your contribution margin on every sale, which increases the exact number of units required to cover fixed overhead — especially on low-ticket items under $25.

What happens when variable cost exceeds net selling price?

Unit contribution margin becomes negative, meaning every additional unit sold loses money. IdeaStress flags break-even as Unreachable instead of outputting a misleading negative number.

Should ad spend be included in fixed overhead for break-even?

If you run a fixed monthly ad budget (e.g., $1,000/mo), include it in fixed overhead to compute true cash break-even; if you scale ad spend per order (CPA), add CPA to variable cost per unit.

Is break-even revenue the same as profit?

No. Break-even revenue is simply the point where total revenue equals total fixed plus variable costs ($0 operating profit before taxes).

03 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is a healthy SaaS LTV:CAC ratio in 2026?

3.0x remains the minimum healthy rule of thumb, while 2026 private SaaS medians sit around 4.1x with an 80% software gross margin and 16-month CAC payback period (Benchmarkit / SubscriptionPlaybook 2026).

What is average monthly churn for low-priced SaaS?

According to ChartMogul's November 2026 benchmarks, median monthly customer churn is 6.2% for SaaS with ARPA under $25, compared to 2.6% for SaaS above $1,000 ARPA.

Why must LTV be gross-margin adjusted?

Using raw revenue instead of gross profit overstates customer value, especially for AI-heavy SaaS products where LLM token COGS compress gross margin well below 80%.

Can LTV be calculated if monthly churn is 0%?

No. Dividing by zero produces infinity; with zero observed churn on a small early cohort, treat LTV as unvalidated until at least 3–6 months of retention data exist.

E-Commerce Margin & Break-Even ROAS

Exact Break-Even ROAS and first-order net profit after COGS, shipping, and Stripe US card fees.

METHODOLOGY & FAQ 3-Step Guide, Exact Formula, Worked Example & 4 FAQs +

  2. Input your blended paid ad CPA / CAC.
  3. Read your exact Break-Even ROAS floor, maximum profitable CPA, and real first-order net profit.
02 · DETERMINISTIC FORMULA & WORKED EXAMPLE
Pre_Ad_Margin = AOV − COGS − Shipping − Payment_Fee
Break_Even_ROAS = AOV / Pre_Ad_Margin
First_Order_Net_Profit = Pre_Ad_Margin − Ad_CPA

On a $68 AOV order with $19 COGS, $7 shipping, and $2.27 Stripe/Shopify fee, pre-ad margin is $39.73 (58.4%). Break-Even ROAS is 68 / 39.73 = 1.71x. At a $34 paid ad CPA, you keep $5.73 first-order net profit.

03 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is Break-Even ROAS in e-commerce?

Break-Even ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) is the minimum ad revenue multiple required so first-order gross profit exactly covers your ad spend. Any ROAS below this floor loses money on every new customer.

What is the average e-commerce CAC in 2026?

First Page Sage benchmarks place blended e-commerce CAC between $53 and $91 across 13 verticals, which is why low-AOV stores (<$40) struggle on paid ads without high repeat purchase rates.

What is a realistic Shopify conversion rate in 2026?

Littledata's late-2026 Shopify dataset reports a 1.4% median store conversion rate, with the top 20% exceeding 2.7% and top 10% exceeding 3.7%.

How do e-commerce gross margins vary by category?

Hycos 2026 benchmarks show 60%–80% for Beauty & Skincare, 55%–70% for Health & Supplements, 45%–65% for Fashion & Apparel, and 15%–30% for Consumer Electronics.

  2. Compare your Base Runway against the built-in Downside Shock (−30% revenue, 50% slower growth, +20% cost overrun).

02 · DETERMINISTIC FORMULA & WORKED EXAMPLE

With $45,000 cash, $3,500 monthly revenue growing 10%/mo, and $8,500 monthly burn, base runway is ~14 months; under a −30% revenue and +20% expense shock, runway compresses sharply to ~6 months.

03 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Why stress-test runway with a −30% revenue and +20% cost shock?

Early startup revenue is lumpy and infrastructure, ad spend, or contractor costs routinely overrun. Planning solely on base-case runway leaves zero buffer when a channel slows down.

What is the difference between gross burn and net burn?

Gross burn is total monthly cash outflow (expenses). Net burn is expenses minus monthly revenue collected. Runway is governed by net burn.

How many months of runway should a bootstrapped founder keep?

Keep at least 6–12 months of shock-tested runway before committing to fixed recurring expenses like full-time hires or annual office leases.

Does revenue growth automatically extend runway?

Only if unit contribution margin is positive and customer acquisition spend does not scale faster than collected cash.
